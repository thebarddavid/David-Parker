# Davina — The Comic

A grounded coming-of-age comic about Davina, an 18-year-old Neko beginning an Arts, Performance and Drama course at a local college.

## Core premise

What happens when the kid who always dreamed of performing finally gets the stage?

Davina has just survived her exams and a frightening college interview. Now she has two years to learn performance, make friends, find her confidence, and discover what she wants from life.

The college is ordinary. The opportunity is extraordinary to her.

## Story principles

- Let the college experience breathe.
- Introduce characters naturally.
- Davina is a person first, not a stereotype.
- Her sexuality is initially private and revealed naturally.
- Romance is slow: friendship → fascination → chemistry → emotional closeness → realisation.
- The future tomboy is introduced as a normal classmate before any romantic framing.
- Davina can be a naturally strong dancer while still being inexperienced at acting.
- Childhood performance memories remain age-appropriate and non-sexual.
- Her final major performance must be earned.

## Current position

Davina has arrived at college and is standing outside her first classroom/studio. The next panel is the classroom door opening.

## Visual continuity

Davina is 6'0", with long vivid red hair, black cat ears, black horns, black cat tail with jewellery and a gold chain, black lipstick, distinctive full-body tattoos, gothic accessories, black platform boots with gold hardware, a gold thigh chain, torn stockings, black shorts, and a small/cropped black top.

Clothing may change between scenes, but her silhouette and identifying features should remain consistent. The tattoos, horns, ears and tail are especially important.
