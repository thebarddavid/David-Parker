# Davina — Artwork

This folder contains the first visual assets for the comic.

## Current artwork

- `davina-cover.svg` — stylised cover illustration establishing Davina's visual identity.
- `chapter-01-page-01.svg` — four-panel storyboard/mock comic page for Chapter 1.

## Visual continuity

Davina is an 18-year-old Neko, 6'0", with long vivid red hair, black cat ears, black horns, a black cat tail with jewellery/gold chain, black lipstick, distinctive full-body tattoos, gothic accessories, black platform boots with gold hardware, a gold thigh chain, torn stockings, black shorts and a cropped black top.

Clothing can change between scenes, but her silhouette and signature features should remain recognisable. Tattoos, horns, ears, tail, hair and jewellery are especially important continuity anchors.

## Art direction

Neon-gothic, expressive, character-focused and grounded. The college should feel like a real UK college rather than a fantasy setting. The artwork should support the coming-of-age story rather than turn Davina into a stereotype.

These SVGs are intentionally editable production assets. They can later be replaced or supplemented with finished illustrations while keeping the same scene and continuity notes.
